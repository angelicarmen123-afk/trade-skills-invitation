Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience?

No. The tasters are designed as beginner-friendly introductions. Follow the instructor's guidance and all safety instructions.

Will I become a qualified tradesperson?

No. A 90-minute taster is an introduction, not a professional qualification or a substitute for formal training or an apprenticeship.

When are the sessions happening?

Dates and venues have not yet been confirmed. Booking is not currently available.

Are the activities supervised?

Activities must be delivered by suitably competent instructors using appropriate equipment, supervision and safety controls.
